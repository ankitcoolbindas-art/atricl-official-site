Maintenance Operations

Spares thinking, like-for-like replacements and follow-up supply after the first project order.

Maintenance for electrical and CCTV infrastructure is mostly about reducing downtime: correct spares, documented replacements, and fast re-supply when a breaker, cable drum section or camera fails. ATRICL supports industrial and commercial buyers in Jharkhand and Bihar with follow-up supply conversations after the first project order.

How we approach maintenance support

  • Like-for-like replacement using nameplate / photo references
  • Spare recommendations when you share a critical-equipment list
  • Coordinated supply for electrical + CCTV when both need attention during a shutdown
  • Clear communication on lead time once the item is identified

What to keep in your plant file

  1. Single-line diagram and DB schedules
  2. Photos of critical MCCB / incomer labels
  3. CCTV channel list and NVR model notes
  4. Preferred brands already approved by your consultant
