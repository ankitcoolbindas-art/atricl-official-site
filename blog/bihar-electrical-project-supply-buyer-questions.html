Bihar Electrical Project Supply — Buyer Questions Checklist

Clear questions for Patna and other Bihar project locations served from ATRICL’s Ranchi operations.

ATRICL is headquartered in Ranchi and serves buyers across Jharkhand and Bihar. If your project is in Bihar — Patna or other cities — ask structured questions so logistics and ratings stay realistic. No invented branch network claims: confirm delivery path on each quote.

Commercial and logistics questions

  • What is the delivery city, gate, and unloading constraint?
  • Is material for stock, a named project, or emergency breakdown?
  • Who is the consignee and who signs packing lists?
  • What quote validity window do you need?
  • Is the order private commercial or GeM-linked?

Technical questions (switchgear / cables / lighting)

  1. Do you have an SLD or DB schedule?
  2. Are makes locked by consultant?
  3. What fault level / kA assumptions exist for MCCBs?
  4. Cable lengths estimated or measure-on-site later?
  5. Lighting mounting heights and IP environment?

CCTV questions for industrial sites

  • Gate + perimeter + indoor coverage priorities
  • IP vs analog preference and network readiness
  • Retention days and NVR channel count
  • Night verification expectations

How ATRICL typically responds

We clarify missing ratings, confirm what we can supply (including owner-confirmed authorised brands), and share a quote path on WhatsApp, phone or email. We will not invent certifications, exclusive territories, or fake client lists.
