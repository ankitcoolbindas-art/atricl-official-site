How Contractors Should Brief a Ranchi Electrical Supplier

Fewer clarification loops, faster usable quotes.

Electrical contractors working from Ranchi across Jharkhand and Bihar often lose hours to incomplete supplier chats. A structured brief helps ATRICL — and any serious supplier — respond with fewer back-and-forth messages.

The 8-line contractor brief (copy/paste)

  1. Site city + gate / landmark
  2. Job type: new / retrofit / breakdown
  3. Shutdown window (date/time) if any
  4. Line list or photo of handwritten schedule
  5. Nameplate photos for replacements
  6. Approved make list (or “open make, suggest options”)
  7. Who unloads and stores material
  8. Commercial path: contractor PO / client direct / GeM-linked

Photos that actually help

  • Existing MCB/MCCB nameplates straight-on, readable
  • DB interior showing neutral/earth bar congestion
  • Cable drum labels when matching like-for-like
  • Gate width if long lengths or large boards arrive

What not to send as the entire enquiry

“Need MCCB urgent rates” without In, poles, or photo forces guessing. Guessing creates wrong material and site idle time.

Brand conversations without over-claiming

If your consultant locked a make list, say so. ATRICL lists authorised partner brands on our brands pages. We do not invent exclusive territory or catalogue claims.
