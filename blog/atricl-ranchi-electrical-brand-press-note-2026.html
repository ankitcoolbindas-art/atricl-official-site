ATRICL Ranchi — Electrical Brand Press Note 2026

Published 5 Oct 2026

Factual brand note for buyers and partners — products, authorised brands, service area, HQ and GSTIN.

ATRICL® is an electrical products and solutions brand based in Ranchi, Jharkhand. Slogan: Smart Power. Infinite Possibilities. Founded in 2024 by Ankit Kumar. GeM-registered supplier. This short public note states what we sell, where we serve, which brands we are authorised for, and how to reach headquarters — without inventing certifications, visitor stats, or extra brand partnerships.

What ATRICL sells

We supply electrical products and solutions for industrial, commercial, contractor and institutional buyers. Core focus is switchgear: ACB, MCCB, MCB, RCCB and changeover. We also supply lighting, cables and wires, and CCTV / surveillance material as part of project and maintenance conversations.

For cables and CCTV we sell supply lines suited to the indent — we do not invent exclusive brand partnerships for those categories. Ask on each enquiry which makes we can support.

Authorised brands

Owner-confirmed authorised brands: C&S Electric, Khul BLDC fan, Greatwhite, Ledure Lighting Ltd, and Jiva. ATRICL is also a C&S Authorised Service Partner for Jharkhand and Bihar. Siemens is available on RFQ-only. Details: authorised brands.

Service area

Headquarters in Ranchi; supply conversations across Jharkhand and Bihar. Delivery logistics depend on item, quantity and transporter norms — confirm on each quote.
