Switchgear: MCB, MCCB & DB

Protection and distribution categories for panels and project BOMs.

Industrial and commercial projects in Jharkhand and Bihar typically buy switchgear as a package: branch protection (MCB), feeder protection (MCCB), and distribution boards that match the panel builder’s layout. ATRICL helps contractors and facility buyers source these categories with clear line-item quotes.

What this hub covers

  • MCBs for final circuits (lighting, power, AC, machinery auxiliaries)
  • MCCBs for incoming / sub-main feeders where higher breaking capacity is required
  • DB / distribution board assemblies and related accessories commonly listed on project BOMs
  • Coordination questions buyers should ask before ordering (poles, curve, kA, incomer size)

Practical buying notes (India projects)

Always match the device to the design load and the fault level assumed by the electrical consultant or panel builder. Do not substitute ratings based only on price. Keep nameplate photos of existing gear when you are replacing like-for-like in a running plant.

For GeM or institutional orders, share catalogue references early so the quote path stays aligned with your procurement format.
