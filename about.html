About ATRICL

Smart Power. Infinite Possibilities. — electrical supply and surveillance solutions from Ranchi across Jharkhand and Bihar.

EST. 2024

ATRICL was founded by Ankit Kumar with a clear focus: reliable electrical supply and practical surveillance solutions for industries, contractors and institutional buyers in eastern India. Headquartered in the Ranchi area, we serve customers across Jharkhand and Bihar.

Our slogan — Smart Power. Infinite Possibilities. — reflects how we work: right products for the load and site, clear communication, and support after the supply conversation starts.

ATRICL is a GeM-registered supplier, which helps government and institutional buyers follow standard procurement pathways for electrical and related categories. We do not invent certifications, partnerships or awards — only facts we can stand behind.
